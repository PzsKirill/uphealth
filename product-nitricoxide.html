The nitric oxide pathway

Three aminos, one job

Your body makes nitric oxide from the amino acid L-Arginine — it relaxes blood vessels so blood, oxygen and nutrients reach your muscles. We pair direct L-Arginine with L-Citrulline, which recycles back into arginine to keep production going, plus a touch of AAKG to support delivery. No stimulants, no proprietary blends.
