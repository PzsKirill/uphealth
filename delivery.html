Shipping options

Standard delivery covers the country in 2 to 4 business days. Express delivery is available in major metros next day for an additional fee.

  • Standard — $6 (free over $80)
  • Express — $14, next business day in major metros
  • Pickup — free at any partnered location
Orders placed before 2 pm local time ship the same day. After 2 pm they go out the next business morning.

Pickup points

We partner with 320+ pickup hubs nationally. Pick the closest one at checkout — most are open seven days a week.

Major metros

  • New York — 28 points
  • Los Angeles — 22 points
  • Chicago — 18 points
  • Houston — 14 points
  • San Francisco — 12 points

International

We currently ship to the EU, UK and Canada. Estimated transit time is 5 to 9 business days depending on customs.

Import duties and taxes are calculated at checkout — no surprises at delivery.

Payment

We accept all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay. For orders over $200, four-payment installments are available at checkout.

  • Cards: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover
  • Wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay
  • Installments: 4x interest-free
  • Invoice on request for business accounts
